About the conference

About IEEE TEMS AIQiA 2027

IEEE TEMS AIQiA 2027 focuses on industrial and emerging frontier technologies, applications of electronics, controls, communications, instrumentation and computational intelligence — with a special emphasis on artificial intelligence and quantum technologies, their innovations and real-world applications.

The objective of the conference is to provide high-quality research and professional interactions for the advancement of science, technology and fellowship. Papers with new research results are encouraged for submission.
