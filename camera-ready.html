Checklist

  1. Address all reviewer comments in the final manuscript.
  2. Format the paper using the IEEE conference template; do not add page numbers.
  3. Validate the PDF with IEEE PDF eXpress (Conference ID: [TBA]).
  4. Complete the IEEE electronic copyright form (eCF).
  5. Register at least one author before the camera-ready deadline.
  6. Upload the final PDF to Microsoft CMT.
